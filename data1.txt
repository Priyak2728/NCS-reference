Employee Leave Policy

Employees receive 12 casual leaves annually.

Maternity leave allows 6 months paid leave.

Unused casual leaves can be carried forward up to 5 days.

Insurance Policy

Medical insurance covers hospitalization up to 5 lakh rupees.

Dental treatment is not covered.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely two days per week.

Manager approval is required.
